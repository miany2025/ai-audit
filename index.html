What the score is made of

Seven weighted groups. Every point must be backed by something found on your site.

Clarity of offer20%Whether a stranger can say what you sell and who it is for after one screen.
Visual consistency15%Colors, type, imagery and layout that look like one brand across pages and channels.
Tone of voice10%Whether the writing sounds like one person or company everywhere.
Trust signals15%Reviews, case studies, named people and policies that a visitor can check.
Differentiation15%How different your promise reads from the competitors you name.
Mobile experience10%Legibility, tap targets and message order on a phone screen.
AI search visibility15%Whether AI assistants mention you, describe you correctly and recommend you. Reported as observations, since their answers change.

How we will handle your site and your data

What we do

  • Read only public pages and respect each site's robots.txt.
  • Never use your content to train AI models.
  • Delete fetched pages and reports after 90 days unless you keep the audit in your account.

What a report cannot do

  • Promise more sales, better rankings or investor interest.
  • Replace legal advice or a trademark search.
  • Predict exactly what AI assistants will say tomorrow.
